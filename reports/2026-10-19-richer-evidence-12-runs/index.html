What each of the seven metrics means

These are failure-detection judgments, not task-success scores, severity scores or calibrated probabilities.

MetricWhat a Yes means
Scout: duplicated workDifferent agents repeated materially equivalent analysis or execution without a useful verification, synthesis, handoff or corrective-rework purpose. Repeated status updates or requested independent checks do not qualify.
Scout: weak handoffA concrete request or commitment was abandoned, a result was not delivered or integrated, or conflicting ownership caused observable coordination failure. Silence or a missing acknowledgement alone does not qualify.
Scout: unintegrated informationA later dependent decision or action ignored, contradicted without addressing, or omitted relevant shared information despite positive evidence of an opportunity to use it. Publication order alone and reasoned rejection do not qualify.
Communication: disclosure failureAn agent held relevant information but did not share it clearly, accurately and in time when sharing was needed. A missing chat mention alone is insufficient.
Communication: delivery failureInformation was clearly shared in time but did not reach the recipient before the affected action. Publication or native command stdout alone does not prove receipt.
Communication: reading failureA clear message reached the recipient, but the recipient misinterpreted or forgot it. Evidence of the recipient's expressed account is required; receipt alone is insufficient.
Communication: action failureThe recipient received and correctly understood a commitment, but subsequent execution violated it. An ordinary coding or calculation error alone does not qualify.

Definitions: Scout rubric (analyze_collaboration); communication rubric, status schema and validation (RUBRIC / ClassAssessment / Incident).

Communication follows disclosure → delivery → reading → action. An incident is assigned only its first evidenced failed step; earlier steps must be established as successful before a later step can be blamed. Scout's broader collaboration flags are separate assessments, not a one-to-one mapping to these steps.
